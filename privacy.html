Privacy Policy for PG MART

Effective Date: May 1, 2026

Last Updated: May 1, 2026

Platform Scope Notice: PG MART operates strictly as a wholesale B2B and consumer delivery platform for dry kirana items, pulses, rice, sugar, grains, and dry fruits in Bhiwandi, Maharashtra, India. The platform explicitly excludes fresh produce, perishables, and vegetables.

1. Information We Collect

To process wholesale orders and facilitate delivery, we collect limited personal data:

  • Account Information: Full name, phone number, and optional email address.
  • Delivery Data: Shipping address, landmark, and local pincode within Bhiwandi.
  • Transaction Records: Order details, order status history, and payment reference numbers. (Payment card details and UPI keys are processed directly by Razorpay and are never stored on PG MART servers).
  • Device Identifiers: Firebase FCM device tokens strictly used to transmit push notifications regarding order status updates.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to:

  • Authenticate users via Phone OTP login.
  • Fulfill and dispatch wholesale orders to designated shipping locations.
  • Send critical transactional updates (Order Confirmation, Out for Delivery, Order Cancelled).
  • Prevent fraudulent transactions and secure account access.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared exclusively with necessary service providers:

  • Google Firebase: Backend cloud hosting, database management, and FCM push notifications.
  • Razorpay: Payment gateway verification and processing.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain order history records as mandated by Indian financial compliance laws. However, users retain the right to request full account deletion at any time.

You can delete your account natively inside the PG MART application under Profile $\rightarrow$ Delete Account, or submit a request online via our Public Account Deletion Web Portal.

5. Security

We implement enterprise-grade security protocols via Google Firebase App Check, encrypted HTTPS data transfer, and strict cloud security rules to protect user information.
